◈ SCAR — Signal Coherence & Acceleration

● 80–100very confident

⚡ PUMP — Pre-earnings Pressure
PUMP = 100·tanh(z·conv/γ), z = abnormal 5-day run-up ÷ the stock's own expected move. PURE PRICE — no Polymarket. Sign = which way it's leaning into the print; |PUMP| = how stretched vs normal. Big + over-extrapolation ⇒ fade (Kelly). Backtestable.
💰 SMG — Market Edge & Stake
Ψ = net edge per $1 = E·trust(SCAR)·uncertainty·regime − friction, then a ½-Kelly stake capped by book depth. Needs a Polymarket price. SCAR says trust; SMG says how much edge & how much to bet.
⚠ SMG defaults are provisional (EV/Kelly + literature) — not yet fit on realized P&L. Validated on the forward vault as bets resolve. Research only; never auto-trades.
📋 FORECAST SHEET — the product
Calibrated probabilities for upcoming earnings. This is the one thing here that passes its own test: on resolved bets our Brier beats the market's. It is a forecast, not a trade — being better calibrated than the crowd is not the same as making money against it.
🏷️ PAPER MAKER — quote, don't lift
We have always been a taker, paying ~4¢ of spread to chase a ~2.6% edge — the spread is 3.1× the edge. This posts fair-value quotes instead and measures what would have filled, above all whether fills arrive precisely when we're wrong (adverse selection). Paper only — no orders, ever.
